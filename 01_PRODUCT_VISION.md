# Product vision

Status: Draft proposal

## Product statement
Neith aims to let a creator or organization produce images, designs, video, motion, audio, and short-form content in one coherent platform, with reusable assets and workflows across specialized apps.

## Intended audiences
Individual creators; small creative teams; agencies managing client brands; enterprise marketing and content operations; automated production operators.

## Jobs to support
Create and edit media; compose mixed-media work; reuse assets, templates, and brand rules; collaborate and review; export and deliver; scale repeatable production.

## Candidate principles
One interoperable project/document foundation; non-destructive edits where feasible; approachable default tools with deeper controls; accessible and performant editing; auditable enterprise workflows; automation using the same production primitives as human work.

## Scope framing
The first release and enterprise rollout are undecided. A complete product vision does not imply that every capability must ship together. Define measurable outcomes and explicit exclusions before committing the roadmap.

## Validation needed
Research target users and priority jobs, competitors, willingness to pay, supported media formats, collaboration expectations, and the first production scenario. Record findings and changes here.
